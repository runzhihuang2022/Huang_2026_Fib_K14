Figure S7. Histological and transcriptomic responses to KLF4 and IGFL1, related to Figures 4 and 6

(A) Representative H&E sections from KLF4-overexpression and overexpression-control groups at 3, 5, 7, 10, and 12 dpb. Black dashed curves with a light outline mark source-annotated epithelial or wound-surface boundaries. Scale bars, 1 mm.

(B) Representative H&E sections from IGFL1 and negative-control groups at the same sampling times. Scale bars, 1 mm.

(C) Gene-wise log2 fold changes after KLF4 overexpression (two treated versus two control libraries) or IGFL1 treatment (two treated versus three PBS libraries) in primary human dermal fibroblasts. Values are from the complementary R/DESeq2 analysis described in STAR Methods. Colors are clipped at ±5; printed values show the untruncated estimates. Asterisks indicate gene-wide BH-adjusted P values: * <0.05, ** <0.01, and *** <0.001. IGFL1 raw counts were zero in all four KLF4-experiment libraries.

(D) Selected preranked GO/KEGG gene-set results for both perturbations. Numbers show normalized enrichment scores (NES); filled symbols indicate FDR <0.05 and open symbols indicate FDR ≥0.05 after BH correction across all tested GO and KEGG sets jointly. Red and blue indicate positive and negative enrichment, respectively. Displayed pathway selection did not alter the correction family.

(E) Patient-level associations between KLF4 and IGFL1 expression (left), and between IGFL1 expression and the wound-keratin module (right), within human Fib_K14. Each point represents one patient (n = 20). Expression is mean log1p(CP10K), averaged across available samples within each patient. The wound-keratin module comprises KRT6A, KRT6B, KRT16, KRT17, LAMC2, LAMB3, and TACSTD2. Spearman coefficients and BH-adjusted P values across the complete tested association family are shown.

(F) Patient-weighted mean percentages of IGFL1- or IGFLR1-detected cells in selected human wound cell states. Numbers in parentheses indicate the available patients for each state. Sample–state groups required at least 20 cells; detection was defined by a nonzero raw count. Patient coverage varies by cell state.

Histological sampling is destructive. The source KFB calibration is approximately 0.221079 micrometers per level-0 pixel; cropped views retain the complete source-annotated wound and uniform x/y scaling. Curves in Figures 4P and 6K use the current section collection described in STAR Methods.
